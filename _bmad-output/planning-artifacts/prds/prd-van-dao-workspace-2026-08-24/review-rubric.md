# PRD Quality Review — Vấn Đạo

## Overall verdict

This is an unusually rigorous PRD for a solo (n=1) project: its thesis — that verification structure, not content, is the differentiator — drives feature grouping, MVP-milestone choice, and success metrics coherently, and most FRs carry numeric, falsifiable bars (κ < 0.6, ≥2 evidence sources, ≥20 calibration samples, max 3 suggestions, no day-based logic) rather than adjectives. The main risks are concentrated in a small number of soft spots — FR1's untested "good enough" book-comprehension bar sitting under the entire pipeline, FR33's unverifiable anti-sycophancy requirement, and an unmonitored external-package dependency (`book-to-skill`) that FR1 depends on — plus a structural note that several sections lean on an external spec doc for full self-containment. None of these threaten the PRD's usefulness as a build-decision artifact; they are refinements, not blockers.

## Decision-readiness — strong

Trade-offs are named with what is given up, not smoothed over. FR11c explicitly accepts a higher disagreement-flag rate in exchange for catching more error types ("tần suất cờ bất đồng có thể cao hơn mức đạt được nếu cho thương lượng lại — đổi lấy khả năng bắt lỗi khác loại"). FR21 requires raw agreement % alongside κ specifically to avoid a known statistical trap (kappa paradox), naming the trade-off's own failure mode. §6's milestone choice is argued against a concrete alternative ("không dừng ở bước 8... mà Soát độc lập chính là khác biệt cốt lõi đã nêu ở §1 Vision") rather than picked for convenience.

§8 Open Questions are genuinely open, each with an Owner and a stated "Điều kiện xem lại" (revisit trigger) rather than a rhetorical question answered in the next sentence. OQ3 is the strongest evidence of honesty here: it carries forward the PRFAQ's self-identified top risk ("crack in the foundation... giải pháp đi tìm vấn đề" — solution looking for a problem) into the PRD as a live, unresolved item rather than quietly dropping it. OQ1 admits real platform lock-in ("kiến trúc hiện tại... gắn khá sâu... chuyển sang nơi khác gần như phải dựng lại phần điều phối") instead of hiding it behind the Non-Goals §5 #6 hedge. No findings.

## Substance over theater — strong

### Findings
- **low** Vision overclaims scope the PRD itself later walks back (§1 vs §9) — Vision states the product turns "bất kỳ cuốn sách PDF/EPUB nào" (any PDF/EPUB book at all) into a guided path, but the Assumptions Index (§9, row 1) admits this is "chưa kiểm với sách có bảng/hình/công thức/code" (untested on books with tables/images/formulas/code). The walk-back is transparent (same document, not buried), so impact is limited, but a reader who stops at §1 forms an uncalibrated impression of validated scope. *Fix:* qualify the Vision sentence ("sách văn bản là chính" or similar) or forward-reference §9's caveat.

Everything else in this dimension reads as earned, not furniture. The single persona (§2) is stated as n=1 with no invented secondary personas. Every FR that claims a mechanism cites a specific source (Roediger & Karpicke 2006, Posner et al. 1982, Sweller & Cooper 1985, Bloom 1968, Deci & Ryan, Pierce/Kostova/Dirks 2003) rather than asserting novelty generically. FR18 actively refuses to borrow credibility from "self-consistency" terminology because the cited technique (Wang et al. 2022) was validated only for closed-answer problems, not subjective grading — this is the opposite of innovation theater. FR23's ritual/gamification system is explicitly gated against crowding-out effects with a cited research basis, pre-empting the obvious "gamification theater" critique. The "Tám vai" (eight roles) glossary entry could have been world-building filler but every role traces to a specific FR group (Thư linh→FR2-7, Giám khảo→FR9, Nghiệm Công Sứ→FR10-12, Phúc Khảo Sứ→FR11a, Chú Giải Sứ→FR29, Sơn phong trưởng lão→FR16-21/FR32, Tàng kinh trưởng lão→FR1, Trưởng môn→FR13-15/FR32).

## Strategic coherence — strong

The thesis is explicit and load-bearing: differentiation is "cấu trúc kiểm chứng" (verification structure), not content (§1), and this is what actually drives the MVP cut. §6 states the milestone directly in thesis terms — stopping at step 8 would leave Dạy (teaching) working but not Soát (independent grading), and grading separation is the stated core differentiator, so MVP must close that loop. This is prioritization that follows from the thesis, not "what's easy first" — note that easier-to-build features (FR13-15 Định hướng, FR30 book-suggestion tool, FR31 visual map) are explicitly deferred to Vòng 3 precisely because they don't yet serve the thesis with only one book in the library.

Success Metrics (§7) validate the thesis rather than measuring activity: "Hoàn thành đột phá quyển thật" is paired with a counter-metric that voids the metric if chapters are completed by skipping grading; "Đạo tâm có bằng chứng vận dụng cụ thể" is paired with a counter-metric that voids pure ritual-logging without real stumble/fix evidence. No DAU/MAU-style activity metric appears (correctly, since n=1 makes such metrics meaningless). MVP scope kind is coherent capability-building for a single user, and the scope logic (defer multi-book, multi-arc, calibration features until there's more than one book to make them meaningful) matches that kind consistently across §6's three deferral tables. No findings.

## Done-ness clarity — strong

The dimension's strength is unusually high: FR16 (≥2 independent evidence sources), FR17/FR20 (κ < 0.6 threshold, ≥20 calibration samples), FR21 (raw % alongside κ), FR27 (1-2 recall questions, explicitly not day-count-based), FR29 (named categories of banned leaked detail: no person/company/project names), and FR30 (max 3 suggestions) are all numerically or behaviorally falsifiable — an engineer could write a pass/fail test directly from the FR text. Applying the rubric's "be unforgiving" instruction surfaces a handful of exceptions:

### Findings
- **high** FR1 has no testable bar for its core claim (§4.1) — "phải giải nghĩa được ý và nội dung cơ bản... đủ để làm nguồn cho việc dạy" ("enough to serve as a source for teaching") is exactly the kind of adjective-grade criterion the rubric flags ("reasonable performance" pattern), and FR1 is the foundation every other FR builds on. The PRD is self-aware about the consequence but doesn't resolve it: the Assumptions Index (§9, row 1) states plainly that "cơ chế hiện tại không kiểm nguồn có đúng với sách gốc, chỉ kiểm người học có làm đúng theo nguồn — lỗi gốc không bị bắt" (the system never checks whether the source interpretation itself is correct — only whether the learner matches it). A wrong book summary could pass every downstream FR undetected. *Fix:* add an explicit acceptance check for FR1 (e.g., a spot-check protocol against source text, or a defined failure mode when source material includes tables/formulas/code per the same assumption row).
- **medium** FR33 (§4.7) has no verification mechanism, unlike comparable behavioral FRs elsewhere in the doc — "Hệ không được khen ngợi mang tính xã giao/nịnh bợ" (no sycophantic praise) gives one example phrase but no eval method, in contrast to FR8 (verified via "Ca đối chứng"), FR18 (verified via dispersion measurement), or FR20 (verified via calibration protocol). The glossary's "Trigger eval / Behavioral eval" entry (§3) is described as a gate for "mọi skill trong PRD" (every skill) but is never explicitly tied to FR33 in text. *Fix:* either reference the Trigger/Behavioral eval mechanism directly from FR33, or state what an anti-sycophancy check looks like in practice.
- **low** Undefined trigger thresholds in two FRs — FR4 (§4.2) requires scaffolding to "tăng lại khi vấp" (increase again on a stumble) without defining what counts as a stumble; FR23 (§4.6) gates rituals on "việc thật đứng sau" (real completed work behind it) without stating the trigger condition per ritual (bái sư vs. thu bí kíp). Both are inferable from context but not stated as a checkable condition. *Fix:* name the trigger condition explicitly for each (e.g., FR4: N consecutive failed attempts; FR23: which system event fires each ritual).

## Scope honesty — strong

Non-Goals (§5) do real work rather than restating the obvious — #6 in particular calibrates certainty precisely, distinguishing "chưa quyết mở rộng" (not yet decided) from "loại trừ vĩnh viễn" (permanently excluded) and tying that calibration back to the PRFAQ's own language. MVP Scope (§6) functions as a de facto `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` mechanism: every deferred FR is listed with the round it moves to and a "Vì sao chưa vào MVP" (why not in MVP) reason — this is more traceable than a prose Non-Goals list would be. The Assumptions Index (§9) is substantive, not decorative: every row states a concrete "Nếu sai thì sao" (consequence if wrong), including uncomfortable ones like FR2-5's pedagogy-transfer assumption ("Đúng quy trình lý thuyết nhưng hiệu quả học thực tế thấp hơn kỳ vọng — chưa có cách đo riêng").

Open-items density (4 Open Questions + a 7-row PRD-local assumptions table, on top of 7 more referenced from the external spec) is high in absolute terms for "Launch" stakes, but each item carries an Owner and revisit trigger, and the PRD's own framing (n=1, author-as-only-user) makes this proportionate rather than sloppy — these read as genuinely tracked risk, not unmanaged scope creep.

### Findings
- **medium** A foundational risk is tracked with less rigor than comparable ones (§9, last row) — the dependency on the external `book-to-skill` package, which FR1 (and therefore the entire Dạy/Soát pipeline) requires to keep working, is noted only in the Assumptions Index ("đã verify chạy thật... nhưng chưa có phương án dự phòng" — verified working but no fallback plan) with no Owner or revisit trigger, unlike OQ1-4 which all get that treatment despite being arguably less structurally central. *Fix:* promote this to a fifth Open Question with an owner and a revisit condition (e.g., "if the package stops being maintained" or "before relying on it beyond MVP").

## Downstream usability — adequate (matters less: standalone PRD)

Per the rubric's own guidance, this dimension carries less weight here since no architecture/story-creation workflow is queued against this PRD yet. For the record: FR numbering is clean and contiguous (FR1-FR33, with FR11a-c and FR12a-b as the only sub-lettering, no gaps or duplicates), and §4's header even documents that independent review lenses already caught and fixed two structural misplacements (FR9/FR20 regrouping) — a good hygiene signal for whenever this does feed downstream work.

### Findings
- **low** Several sections are not fully self-extractable without a companion document — §6's MVP tables, §4's FR annotations (F-1, F0, F3-F5′, F6-F9 labels), and §9's assumptions all point to `docs/VAN-DAO-dac-ta-v1.0.md` (§16 step numbers, §17 assumption list, R-numbers) for grounding that isn't restated in the PRD. This is a deliberate, stated choice (§3: "không chép lại ở đây... chỉ trích dẫn khi cần") to avoid duplicating the spec, which is reasonable for a solo working document, but it means a section pulled out alone (per the rubric's downstream-usability test) would need that external doc to be fully meaningful. Low severity given the standalone framing. *Fix:* none needed now; if this PRD is later handed to a downstream workflow, inline the specific step/assumption text being referenced rather than just the label.

## Shape fit — strong

The PRD is capability-spec shaped, matching a single-operator tool correctly: no UJs, FRs organized by capability group (Thu sách, Dạy, Soát, Định hướng, Đo, Nền, cross-cutting), and Success Metrics that are self-observed rather than dashboard-based ("tác giả tự quan sát từ đạo tâm và trải nghiệm của chính mình" — §7), appropriate given Non-Goal #3 (no telemetry). This is neither over-formalized (no UJ overhead forced onto a solo tool) nor under-formalized (the "Launch" stakes and secondary open-source audience justify the unusually high rigor — academic citations, calibration protocol, explicit glossary — that a pure hobby project wouldn't need). The PRD is also self-aware about the over-engineering risk this shape invites: OQ1, OQ3, and Non-Goal #6 all directly interrogate whether the eight-role/cultivation-framework investment is justified for n=1, rather than assuming it without question. No findings.

## Mechanical notes

- **No inline bracket-tag convention used.** The document contains no literal `[ASSUMPTION: ...]`, `[NOTE FOR PM]`, or `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` tags anywhere in the body text; §4's FRs use parenthetical `(nguồn: ...)` / `(lý do: ...)` citations instead, and the equivalent functions are served by dedicated sections (§9 Assumptions Index, §8 Open Questions with Owner/revisit columns, §6 MVP deferral tables with reason columns). This is functionally adequate but means the roundtrip the rubric checks for (every inline assumption tag indexed, every index entry traceable inline) doesn't apply in its literal form — a reader scanning FR1-33 has no forward-pointer telling them FR1 rests on an unverified assumption; they'd only learn that by separately reading §9.
- **Glossary drift:** none found in spot-checks. Bí kíp/Quyển, Cảnh giới/Bậc, Nhãn/Dấu hiệu, and the "Tám vai" role names are all used consistently between §3 and their corresponding FRs.
- **ID continuity:** FR1-FR33 contiguous, no gaps or duplicates; sub-lettering (FR11a-c, FR12a-b) is consistent between §4 and the §6 MVP tables. Non-Goals (1-6) and Open Questions (1-4) are both contiguous.
- **Cross-references:** F-label (F-1, F0, F3-F5′, F6-F9) and R-number citations are used consistently between §4 and §6, but point to an external doc (`docs/VAN-DAO-dac-ta-v1.0.md`) not provided alongside this PRD, so they could not be independently verified here.
- **UJ protagonist naming:** N/A — no UJs present, consistent with the capability-spec shape (see Shape fit).
- **Required sections:** Vision, Audience, Glossary, Features/FRs, Non-Goals, MVP Scope, Success Metrics, Open Questions, and Assumptions Index are all present — the full complement expected for "Launch" stakes in a capability-spec-shaped PRD. No dedicated NFR/UX section exists, but NFR-equivalent content (κ thresholds, subagent isolation, fork prohibition) is embedded directly in the relevant FRs, which fits this PRD's shape.
